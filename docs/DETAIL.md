# Niuma Broker · Make finding the right job effortless

> Your resume is the least important link in the chain. Knowing your target, sensing the real temperature of the market, and feeding market demand back into what you invest in today and where your career heads next—all three matter far more than the resume itself.
>
> Put differently: when you keep researching, trying new things, and adjusting your judgment from real feedback to nail those three, it is hard for your resume *not* to stand out.
>
> So even though the entry point of this product is your resume, it is only a starting point—an initial signal. Every core capability is built around those three things:

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Define your target and find roles that actually fit
- Enable **career board patrols** to keep subscribed public job listings up to date. Get a short match rationale and see which gaps to pay attention to.
- **AI Deep Read & Red Flags**: Break down strengths and scope leaps against your track record, paired with ready-to-ask interview questions and direct jumps back to the original JD.
- **Application tracking** brings New → Applied → Interview → Offer into one view, with sync to Calendar, Reminders or Things on macOS and `.ics` export.

### 2. Sense the market temperature and real hiring demand
- The **capability radar and skill quadrant** compare active job listings with your resume and work achievements. Background insights use finalized resumes only.
- **Actionable positioning plan**: Turn strengths and high-demand gaps into weekly, monthly, and quarterly moves—linked to weaving wins into your resume, filtering matching JDs, or starting a DSH study session.
- **Company & geography pulse**: See hiring density across tracks, Senior/Staff share, city distribution, and posting velocity from live openings.

### 3. Shape your daily work and capture high-value wins
- **Work achievement capture** uses your own messages in authorized DSH workspaces to extract achievements with supporting excerpts. Review the details and metrics in the pending queue before marking them shareable.
- **Multi-master & role-tailored branches**: Evolve your master resume alongside role-specific branches, sync updates cleanly from master, and edit via chat, Tab autocomplete, and live A4 PDF export.

> **In the end, everything falls into place naturally. To every builder and rising talent out there—wishing you a role you genuinely want to do!**

## Notes

- The plugin stores its **state** on your machine under `~/.dsh/profiles/<profile>/career-broker/`. AI features send relevant context to the model provider you configure in DSH. Deep tasks and Tab autocomplete can use separate models and reasoning settings.
- **Opt-In & Scoped**: Background work distillation, career board patrols, and auto-analysis on open all start **OFF** until enabled. Authorize only personal or sanitized workspace folders—never send NDA-restricted confidential material. Never simulates logins or auto-applies.
